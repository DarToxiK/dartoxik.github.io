claude-google-local

A private, personal tool. It lets its owner use an AI assistant (Claude) on their own Google accounts: read and organise their own Gmail, read their own Google Drive, and manage their own Google Calendar.

  • It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.
  • It runs on the owner's own computer and talks directly to Google's APIs.
  • No data is sold, shared or stored on any third-party server.
